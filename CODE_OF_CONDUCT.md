# Code of Conduct / 协作行为规范

This policy applies to project Issues, Discussions, pull requests and other project-managed conversations. People with different experience levels and languages are welcome.

本规范适用于项目的 Issues、Discussions、PR 和其他项目管理的交流空间，欢迎不同经验和语言背景的参与者。

## Expectations / 共同约定

- Discuss the work, not a person's worth or identity. Explain disagreements with examples and evidence.
- Be patient with questions and corrections. Keep feedback relevant and actionable.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's identity, contact details, credentials or private conversations.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, direct unwanted sexual attention at others, or repeatedly spam or disrupt discussion.

讨论代码与事实，不攻击人格或身份；对提问与纠正保持耐心，反馈应具体可执行。尊重隐私和同意，不公开他人的个人信息、凭据或私密交流。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视、不受欢迎的性关注、刷屏及持续扰乱讨论。

## Concerns and moderation / 反馈与管理

Use GitHub's reporting controls on offending content, or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/), for harassment, threats or exposed personal information. Do not repeat private evidence in a public thread. Software vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

遇到骚扰、威胁或个人信息泄露，可使用相关内容上的 GitHub 举报功能或 GitHub Support；不要在公开讨论中再次传播私密证据。软件漏洞请使用独立的安全报告流程。

Maintainers may ask for an edit, remove disruptive content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation when needed. Decisions should consider the behavior, its impact and repeated violations, and apply the same expectations to everyone, including maintainers.

维护者可要求修改、移除扰乱内容、关闭或锁定讨论，必要时限制参与。处理应考虑行为、影响与是否重复违反规则；相同要求也适用于维护者。
